# Privacy Policy

**App Name: Seenafy**  
**Last Updated: September 9, 2026**

Seenafy (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be accessed, used, and processed when you use Seenafy and its integrations with third-party platforms.

## 1. Information We Access

Depending on the features you use, Seenafy may access information from connected third-party services, including:

- Account information necessary to authenticate your account.
- Information about Pins, boards, and other content available through the connected account.
- Information required to create, publish, manage, or analyze content.
- Technical information necessary to operate the application and its integrations.

Seenafy only accesses information necessary to provide the functionality requested by the user.

## 2. How We Use Information

We may use accessed information to:

- Authenticate and maintain connections with third-party services.
- Create, publish, manage, and organize content.
- Retrieve account, board, Pin, or performance information requested by the user.
- Operate, maintain, and improve Seenafy.
- Detect, prevent, and address security or technical issues.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information.

## 3. Third-Party Services

Seenafy may connect to third-party platforms and APIs, including Pinterest and other services selected by the user.

When you connect a third-party account, the relevant information is processed according to both this Privacy Policy and the privacy policies of the applicable third-party service.

Seenafy does not control how third-party platforms independently process information. Users should review the privacy policies of those services.

## 4. Authentication and API Access

When you authorize Seenafy to connect to a third-party service, the service may provide authentication credentials or access tokens.

These credentials are used only to provide the functionality authorized by the user. We take reasonable measures to protect authentication information from unauthorized access.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's functionality, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, or fulfill another legitimate purpose described in this Privacy Policy.

When information is no longer required, we may delete or securely dispose of it.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

- Access information held about you.
- Request correction of inaccurate information.
- Request deletion of information.
- Restrict or object to certain processing.
- Withdraw consent where applicable.

Requests may be submitted through the contact method provided by Seenafy.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Seenafy is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where prohibited by applicable law.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date.

## 10. Contact

A contact method for privacy-related requests will be provided by Seenafy when available.
