# Privacy Policy

**App Name: Seenafy**  
**Last Updated: September 9, 2026**

Seenafy is a personal automation application designed to automate content management and publishing across connected online platforms using artificial intelligence, including Claude by Anthropic.

## 1. Purpose of Seenafy

Seenafy is designed to automate tasks such as:

- Creating and preparing content.
- Publishing content to connected platforms.
- Managing and organizing content.
- Retrieving information from connected accounts.
- Automating repetitive social media and content management tasks.

Seenafy is intended for personal use and is not designed as a public consumer service.

## 2. Information Accessed

Seenafy may access information from platforms that the user explicitly connects to the application.

This may include account information, content, boards, Pins, publishing information, analytics, and other information made available through the relevant platform's API.

Seenafy only accesses information necessary to perform the automation requested by the user.

## 3. Use of Information

Information accessed through connected platforms may be used to:

- Automate content creation and publishing.
- Manage connected accounts and their content.
- Organize content.
- Retrieve analytics and account information.
- Execute actions requested by the user.
- Improve the operation and reliability of the automation system.

Seenafy does not sell, rent, or otherwise commercially distribute personal information.

## 4. Artificial Intelligence

Seenafy may use artificial intelligence services, including Claude by Anthropic, to assist with content creation, organization, analysis, and automation.

Information sent to an AI service is limited to the information necessary for the requested automation and functionality.

## 5. Third-Party APIs

Seenafy may connect to third-party platforms through their official APIs.

These platforms may include Pinterest and other services selected and authorized by the user.

When a user connects an account, authentication and access are handled through the relevant third-party authorization process.

The use of third-party services is also subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

## 6. Authentication

Seenafy may use access tokens or other authentication credentials provided by connected platforms to perform authorized actions.

These credentials are used only to provide the functionality explicitly authorized by the user.

## 7. Data Retention

Seenafy does not intentionally retain personal information beyond what is reasonably necessary to operate its automation features.

Information may be temporarily processed or stored when required to perform an automated task, maintain a connection, or troubleshoot technical issues.

## 8. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information and authentication credentials handled by Seenafy.

However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

## 9. User Control

The user controls which third-party accounts are connected to Seenafy and which permissions are granted.

The user may revoke access to a connected platform at any time through that platform's account or authorization settings.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the functionality of Seenafy changes.

Any updates will be published on this page with a new “Last Updated” date.

## 11. Contact

Seenafy is currently operated as a personal automation application.

A dedicated contact method will be provided when available.
